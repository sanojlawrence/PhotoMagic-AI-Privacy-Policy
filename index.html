2. Information We Collect

When you create or sign in to a PhotoMagic AI account, we may collect information associated with your account, including:

2.2 Photos and Images

PhotoMagic AI allows you to select images using the Android system photo picker.

When you choose an image for an AI feature:

  • The selected image may be uploaded to our cloud infrastructure for processing.
  • The image may be transmitted to third-party AI service providers used by PhotoMagic AI to perform the requested AI processing.

  • PhotoMagic AI does not use your uploaded images for advertising purposes.

PhotoMagic AI does not directly access your device camera.

PhotoMagic AI uses the Android system photo picker to allow you to select photos or images for processing.

PhotoMagic AI generates images as part of its AI features.

Generated images are provided to you as part of the requested service. PhotoMagic AI does not permanently store generated images on its own servers as part of the normal service.

Temporary processing data may exist while a request is being processed or while required for the service to complete.

This may include information such as:

13. Account Deletion and Data Deletion

PhotoMagic AI allows users to request deletion of their account and associated personal data.

Users can submit an account deletion request through the PhotoMagic AI application. Users may also submit a deletion request through the external account-deletion request method provided by PhotoMagic AI.

Deletion Request Process

When an account deletion request is submitted:

  1. The request is recorded for processing.
  2. The request is scheduled for processing within 7 days.
  3. During this period, the request may be reviewed to prevent accidental, fraudulent, or unauthorized deletion requests.
  4. Once the request is processed, the PhotoMagic AI account and associated data will be permanently deleted, subject to information that we are legally required or otherwise permitted to retain.
Important: Once deletion has been completed, deleted account data cannot be recovered.

Certain information may need to be retained for a limited period where required for:

  • Legal compliance
  • Fraud prevention
  • Security
  • Financial records
  • Dispute resolution
  • Other legitimate legal requirements

Where information is retained for these reasons, it will be handled in accordance with applicable requirements.

Account Deletion Request by Email

Users may request deletion by contacting:

enquiry.jlplayer@gmail.com

Please use the subject line:

PhotoMagic AI Account Deletion Request

Please include the email address associated with your PhotoMagic AI account.

We may use reasonable measures to verify the identity and authenticity of a deletion request before processing it.

In-App Account Deletion Request

PhotoMagic AI provides an account-deletion request option within the application.

This option submits a deletion request; it does not immediately delete the account or associated data.
