What happens when you request deletion?

When you submit an account deletion request, your request will be reviewed and processed within 7 days.

Once your request is processed, your PhotoMagic AI account and associated data will be permanently deleted, subject to information that we are legally required or otherwise permitted to retain.

Important

Account deletion is permanent. Once your account and associated data have been deleted, the deleted data cannot be recovered.

What may be deleted?

Depending on the data associated with your account, the deletion process may include:

  • Your PhotoMagic AI account
  • Account-related information
  • Associated application data
  • Associated user-generated application data
  • Other account-associated data that we control

Information that we are required or permitted to retain for legal, financial, security, fraud-prevention, or dispute-resolution purposes may be retained for the applicable period.
